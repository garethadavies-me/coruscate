About TeamPickr

TeamPickr is a mobile app that helps organisers create groups, manage players, and quickly generate fair, balanced football teams based on player ability ratings and availability.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create my first group?

In the app, go to the groups tab, tap “Create group”, give your group a name, then start adding players with their ability ratings.

How are teams generated?

TeamPickr uses player ability ratings and availability to automatically generate two balanced teams. You can adjust ratings over time to improve balance.

How do I report a bug or issue?

If something isn't working as expected, please email us with details of what happened, which device you’re using, and (if possible) screenshots. This helps us fix issues more quickly.
